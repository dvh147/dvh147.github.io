Research Notes

Research Notes is a growing collection of short literature reviews on the economics of innovation, each arguing a single narrow claim from cited papers. This is a little side project where I let a research agent find, read, and analyze research papers on a given topic. As this is heavily AI-reliant, I generally do not trust the output blindly, but I do like reading the posts for inspiration.
